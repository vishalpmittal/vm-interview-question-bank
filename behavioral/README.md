# Behavioral Interview Questions

Question bank for engineering IC behavioral interviews. All questions are consolidated in a single file, organized by competency area with section-level evaluation guidance and STAR-method scoring.

## How to Use

Pick 2-3 competency areas relevant to the role and level. Select 1-2 questions per area. Use the section-level "What to Listen For," "Follow-ups," and "Red Flags" to guide evaluation.

## Competency Areas

- **Collaboration & Teamwork** — Working across teams, giving/receiving feedback, navigating disagreements
- **Communication** — Explaining technical concepts, writing, influencing without authority
- **Conflict Resolution** — Technical disagreements, interpersonal friction, escalation judgment
- **Delivering Results** — Ownership, prioritization, shipping under constraints
- **Learning & Growth** — Learning from failure, skill development, adapting to new domains
- **Navigating Ambiguity** — Handling unclear requirements, changing priorities, incomplete information
- **Problem-Solving & Adaptability** — Debugging production issues, creative solutions, responding to surprises
- **Technical Decision-Making** — Trade-off analysis, choosing approaches, defending decisions

## STAR-Method Evaluation

When evaluating answers, assess each component:

| Component | Strong Signal | Weak Signal |
|-----------|--------------|-------------|
| **Situation** | Specific context with stakes and constraints | Vague or hypothetical scenario |
| **Task** | Clear ownership and scope of their responsibility | Unclear what was theirs vs. the team's |
| **Action** | Concrete steps they personally took | "We did X" without clarifying their role |
| **Result** | Measurable outcome or specific impact | No resolution or only positive spin |

A strong answer names specifics: team size, timeline, what broke, what they tried, what happened. A weak answer stays abstract and uses "we" throughout without distinguishing personal contribution.
