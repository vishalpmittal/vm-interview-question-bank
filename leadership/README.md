# Leadership Interview Questions

Question bank for engineering leadership and management interviews (tech leads, EMs, senior+ managers). Questions are consolidated in a single file, organized by competency area with section-level evaluation guidance.

## How to Use

Pick 2-3 competency areas relevant to the role and level. Select 1-2 questions per area. Use the section-level "What to Listen For," "Follow-ups," and "Red Flags" to guide evaluation.

## Question Bank

- [leadership-questions.md](leadership-questions.md)

## Competency Areas

- **People Management & Performance** — Poor performers, difficult high performers, time-consuming people problems
- **Coaching, Development & 1:1s** — Growth, retention, structuring feedback loops
- **Team Leadership & Motivation** — Leading through demoralizing events, performance dips, reorgs
- **Org Strategy & Alignment** — Cascading strategy, surfacing misalignment between team and company goals
- **Managing Up & Cross-Level Dynamics** — Evaluating EMs, handling skip-level escalations about you
- **Leadership Philosophy & Approach** — How you coach, run 1:1s, set goals, manage performance, prioritize, measure success
- **Hiring, Team Building & Credibility** — Hiring process, building high-performing teams, stepping into inherited teams
- **Self-Awareness & Reflection** — Strengths/weaknesses, feedback received, best/worst teams, project failures
- **Motivation & Role Fit** — Why this company, what you want to work on, mission resonance

## File Conventions

- One markdown file per competency area or consolidated by role scope.
- Each section follows: numbered question list, **What to listen for**, **Follow-ups**, **Red flags**.
